HomeTask 14.4 — Add 5% Customer Service Fee

IMPORTANT: Back up your entire HomeTask project first, especially hometask.sqlite3.
This patch is based on the recovered HomeTask 14.3 sources and uses the original 14.3 design.

1. Stop your local server (Control+C in Terminal).
2. Copy the four main files from this patch into your existing HomeTask directory:
   server.py, app.js, styles.css, index.html
3. DO NOT replace or delete your existing hometask.sqlite3 or brand/ folder.
4. Restart from your project directory:
   python3 -m uvicorn server:app --host 0.0.0.0 --port 8000
5. Open http://127.0.0.1:8000 and hard refresh with Command+Shift+R.

New task bookings will display a 5% customer service fee in addition to the agreed task price. The backend creates an additive schema migration; old payment records retain their previous customer total and zero fee.

With no Stripe test credentials, bookings are explicitly unfunded demos. Stripe TEST checkout uses test cards only. Live payments, real payouts and Stripe Connect are NOT enabled.

The archive deliberately excludes brand files, database files, passwords and other private data.
