# HomeTask 15.13.0 — Customer Cards and Ribbon Placement

- Removed redundant customer-home next-steps card; existing Open listings, In progress and Upcoming appointments stats remain clickable.
- Standardized My Tasks page to two columns with an independently scrolling list, preserving management controls.
- Enlarged/centered category icons for tasks with no photos.
- Reduced customer-home job cards to one offer-count badge; removed redundant Compare offers buttons from cards, while leaving the cards clickable.
- Added a New offer marker based on offer-count changes since the customer last opened that task's offer comparison (tracked in the current browser session).
- Restored top-left preview-card ribbons and moved the NEW hanging ribbon to the top-right of the open task details modal, not the bottom of the offer form.
- Save/unsave actions within job details no longer close/reopen the modal.
- Preserves 15.12.9 rounded profile tiles, proposal styling, and worker homepage improvements.
