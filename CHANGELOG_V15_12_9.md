# HomeTask 15.12.9 — Dashboard & Offer Experience Refinement

## Customer homepage
- Removed redundant Compare New Offers and Current Offers summary prompts.
- Combined the task overview and offer discovery into a two-column grid showing two rows at a time.
- Additional open/active customer tasks scroll vertically inside the section, with corrected card photo proportions.
- Each task shows its real offer count. Cards with open offers open offer comparison; other tasks open normal task details.
- Preserved See All Tasks, dashboard statistics, and important completion alerts.

## Worker homepage
- Balanced the Personalized Matches and Worker Tools columns by adding a compact My Work at a Glance panel beneath the matching preferences.
- The new panel uses actual completed job and submitted offer counts, not fictional metrics.

## Job offers and saved ribbons
- Restyled the worker offer/proposed schedule form with lighter sections, more polished input grouping, and a clearer payout breakdown. Existing form IDs, validation and payment calculations are unchanged.
- Rounded the clickable worker profile tiles and their hover shadows in the offer comparison screen.
- Moved the hanging ribbon to the top-right of worker job cards; kept the translucent unsaved state, yellow saved state, and save-without-carousel-reset logic.

No backend or database changes.
