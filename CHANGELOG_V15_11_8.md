# HomeTask 15.11.8 — Worker Home Featured Jobs

- Replaces the first-four vertical featured list with a horizontally scrolling rail of actual open HomeTask job cards.
- Prioritizes jobs that match worker skills and nearby radius. Also includes other eligible open listings rather than limiting results to 4–6.

- Keeps Worker Tools below the carousel and adds a job preferences tip.
